============================
Astrid's Guide to Necromancy
============================

So, you want to raise the dead.

My name is Astrid, apprentice to Mannimarco, and this is my guide. Within these pages, you will find ten reanimation spells and eight supporting incantations. Some spells will consume corpses, leaving only loot piles behind. Raise the dead, explode their remains, and bargain with darkness as you tread the path of the necromancer.

============================
Installation instructions
============================
Download the mod for your operating system, and unzip it into your StreamingAssets folder.

============================
Main features
============================

After character creation, you will find Astrid’s Guide to Necromancy in your inventory. When you read it, you will receive every spell you currently qualify for. As you level up, you can reread the guide to gain access to even stronger undead.
All spells added by this mod are Mysticism school.

Reanimation
There are nine Reanimate spells that each raise one specific type of undead, plus a tenth - simply named "Reanimate" - that automatically raises the strongest type your current level and Magicka can sustain, falling back to something cheaper if you can't yet afford anything stronger. All ten require a nearby corpse to cast. Reanimated allies follow you, engage hostile creatures they notice nearby on their own (not just whatever you've personally struck), teleport back to you if left too far behind, and expire after their Duration runs out. You will gain the ability to create the following undead at these specific levels:

Level 1: Skeletal Warriors
Level 2: Zombies
Level 3: Ghosts
Level 5: Mummies
Level 7: Wraiths
Level 9: Vampires
Level 12: Ancient Vampires
Level 13: Liches
Level 16: Ancient Liches

Legion Size
You can control a number of undead that fits into your party budget (maximum spell points + your mysticism score). More powerful undead require a greater share of your legion's budget, limiting how many you can command. With advanced enchanting, you can dramatically expand this limit—potentially enough to become a true lich king. (maximum spell points + your mysticism score). The Dangers of Necromancy
When you call upon reanimate  the dead, there is a chance your creation will not recognize you as its master and attack. As you gain levels, that chance gets smaller and smaller, until it goes away completely. Your subjugate spell can bring hostile undead back under your control. You can toggle hostile creations off in the mod settings if you would prefer your undead to always be obedient.

Corpse Explosion
Sometimes you just need to explode a corpse, dealing Fire damage to everything nearby equal to that creature's typical health pool.

Deathknell
You can consume a corpse to restore your own health and stamina, each equal to half that creature's typical health pool.

Subjugate Undead
When you inevitably raise something you can't handle, this spell will be your lifeline. It grants you a chance to bend hostile undead back to your will - including ones you didn't raise yourself, found wandering the world - adding them to your legion just like a fresh Reanimation. It draws on your upkeep budget the same way, oldest-servant culling included.

Dismiss Undead
When the dead have served their purpose, this spell will dispose of them for you.

Volatile Undead
A servant is often more useful once sacrificed. This spell detonates one of your own allied undead, dealing Fire damage to everything nearby equal to whatever health it had remaining - sparing you and the rest of your legion.

Command Undead
Having a undead horde is pointless if they are not there when you need them. This spell instantly brings your legion to your side to engage your foes. 

Dark Deal
This spell allows you to trade your own lifeforce for power: a tenth of your maximum health, for a fifth of your maximum magicka.

Seance
When you are having trouble finding that quest target behind a secret door, you can call upon those you have slain to show you the way. After you have killed at least 10 enemies in a dungeon, you gain the ability to summon a spirit that will move directly towards the quest target. He will show you levers you need to pull, descend pits, and open secret doors. 

Custom Ambient Sounds
There is nothing that will make you regret necromancy more than listening to a group of skeletons scream at you. To solve this, this mod adds several custom undead sounds to make the experience much less screamy.

New Console Commands
embrace_darkness: Adds every spell this mod directly to your spellbook, regardless of level.

astrid_book: Adds Astrid's Guide to Necromancy to your inventory, if you don't already have it.

Necromancy is a Crime
Casting any spell from this mod while in a town is treated a crime on par with murder. You can toggle this feature off in the settings.

============================
Compatibility
============================

Daggerfall Enemy Expansion by kaboissonneault

If you play with Daggerfall Enemy Expansion (a fantastic mod that greatly increases enemy variety), you can reanimate the extended undead. Every new creature added by the expansion is supported by our corpse-consuming abilities.
